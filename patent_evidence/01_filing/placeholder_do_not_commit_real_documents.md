# ⚠️ DO NOT COMMIT REAL DOCUMENTS HERE

This directory (`01_filing/`) is for **placeholder and checklist** files only.

## Prohibited Content

- ❌ Real filing receipts (受理通知书)
- ❌ Real application numbers (申请号)
- ❌ Attorney correspondence (代理沟通记录)
- ❌ Client data (客户信息)
- ❌ Third-party confidential materials (第三方保密材料)
- ❌ Fee payment receipts (缴费凭证)
- ❌ CNIPA official notifications (国知局官方通知)

## What CAN Be Here

- ✅ Filing checklist templates
- ✅ Placeholder documents
- ✅ Process notes (without confidential details)

## If You Need to Store Real Documents

Store them in a **separate private repository** with appropriate access controls, or in a secured local directory outside this repository.
